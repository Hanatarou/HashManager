Kowane algorithm yana aiki a zarensa kuma karantawa yana haɗuwa da lissafi, saboda haka hash da yawa suna kashe kusan daidai da mafi jinkiri. Babban fayil, Tabbatarwa da Kwafi suma suna lissafa fayiloli da yawa a lokaci guda (Zaɓuɓɓuka > Lissafa fayiloli da yawa a lokaci guda; a kashe don diski mai tauri).\n\nHYPER-THREADING\nSaiti ne na BIOS/UEFI wanda shiri ba zai iya canzawa ba. Zaɓuɓɓuka > Yi amfani da zaren Hyper-Threading yana iyakance yawan zaren hash da ke aiki a lokaci guda kawai: a kashe = ɗaya a kowane ainihin cibiya. Sandar matsayi tana nuna cibiyoyi, zare da ko Hyper-Threading yana kunne.\n\nFAYIL\nZaɓi ko jefa fayil. Manna hash da ake tsammani (ko yi amfani da Manna daga allon kwafi): ana kwatanta shi da kowane hash da aka lissafa, kuma idan tsawonsa bai dace da kowane algorithm da aka zaɓa ba, shirin yana faɗa maka wanda za ka zaɓa. Entropy (8 = bayanai na bazuwar ko matsattse) da ma'aunin Zstandard na zaɓi ne.\n\nBABBAN FAYIL\nYana lissafa duk fayiloli, tare da ƙananan manyan fayiloli idan an zaɓa. Ana tsallake symlinks. Ajiye fayil ɗin checksum (layi ɗaya ga kowane fayil, 'hash *hanya', hanyoyi da /; ga CRC-32 .sfv na gaske). Idan an zaɓi algorithm da yawa, zaɓi wanda fayil ɗin checksum zai yi amfani da shi.\n\nRUBUTU\nDukan rubutu, layi bayan layi (ana tsallake layuka marasa komai) ko Base64 (ana lissafa hash na bytes da aka warware). Ana sarrafa rubutu a matsayin UTF-8, yadda aka rubuta shi daidai.\n\nKWATANTA\nFayiloli biyu: dole duk hash da aka zaɓa su zama daidai. Manyan fayiloli biyu: ana haɗa fayiloli ta hanya mai dangantaka kuma a ba da rahoto a matsayin Daidai, Banbanci, A kawai ko B kawai.\n\nTABBATAR DA CHECKSUM\nYana karanta .sha256, .md5, .sfv, SHA256SUMS da makamantansu (GNU 'hash *fayil', BSD 'SHA256 (fayil) = hash' da SFV). Hanyoyi suna dangane da fayil ɗin checksum; hanyar da aka rubuta da \\ ana samunta a Linux da macOS ma. Idan layi bai faɗi algorithm ɗinsa ba, ana amfani da sunan fayil ɗin checksum (.b3, B3SUMS, .sha3, SHA512SUMS...) sannan tsawon hash. Sakamako: YA YI, BAI DACE BA, BABU ko AN tsallake.\n\nKWAFI\nAna fara haɗa fayiloli ta girma; waɗanda girmansu ɗaya ne kaɗai ake lissafa hash ɗinsu. Ana watsi da fayiloli marasa komai, da hard links zuwa bayanai iri ɗaya. Sharewa na dindindin ne (babu kwandon shara), yana farawa ba tare da wata alama ba, kuma shirin yana ƙin share duk kwafin fayil.\n\nDISKI\nYana jera kowane diski da samfuri, girma, nau'i (SSD, diski mai tauri, NVMe, USB...), ko mai cirewa ne da volumes ɗinsa. Karanta diski yana buƙatar haƙƙin mai gudanarwa (Windows) ko root (Linux, macOS); ana karanta diski ne kawai, ba a taɓa rubutawa, kuma ana sarrafa dukan diski, har da wurin da ba shi da fayiloli.\n\nHARSHE\nFayil > Harshe. Harsuna 20 da aka fi magana da su; ana ajiye zaɓi a HashManager.ini. Ana rubuta Larabci da Urdu daga dama zuwa hagu. Fayil > Alamar decimal. Bi tsarin, Digo (1.5), Waƙafi (1,5).\n\nSAITUNA DA RAJISTA\nAna ajiye su idan an rufe shirin, a HashManager.ini kusa da shirin (mai ɗaukuwa: babu mai shigarwa, ba a rubuta komai a wani wuri).
